# Reference Pack 2 — Jakarta Urban Asset Expansion

Visual reference pack for Indonesian open-world browser game *Dude Theft Three* (`~/Code/dude-theft-three`).
Every asset in this set is curated from Wikimedia Commons under permissive open licences (**CC0**, **CC BY**, or **CC BY-SA**) and downloaded locally to `reference/assets/`.

This pack covers five core Indonesian urban infrastructure categories:
1. **Masjid** — Jakarta neighbourhood mosque (contemporary urban dome + minaret, human scale)
2. **Pasar** — Traditional market hall (PD Pasar Jaya style pasar rakyat / pasar induk facade and stalls)
3. **Terminal Bus** — Indonesian bus terminal shelter, boarding bays, and multi-modal fleet (Metrotrans & Mikrotrans/angkot)
4. **Kurir / Delivery** — App-based motorbike courier with large insulated rear cargo box
5. **Pos Ronda** — Traditional neighbourhood security post / gardu siskamling with kentongan

- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 4.0

- **Original Dimensions:** 3000 x 4000 pixels (3.08 MB)

**Modeller Reproduction Guide:**
Front three-quarter elevated exterior view of a contemporary Jakarta neighbourhood mosque (Masjid Al-Musyawarah, Kelapa Gading). 3D modellers should reproduce the distinctive central ribbed hemispherical dome in vivid turquoise/cyan enamel topped with an ornamental gilded brass crescent finial, accompanied by a slender octagonal minaret tower that features an open cantilevered muezzin balcony and a miniature turquoise cone roof. The main building mass is a two-storey cubic whitewashed concrete structure wrapped with decorative geometric solar breeze-block screens (*roster* / *brise-soleil*) across upper clerestory zones and dark tinted bronze-framed arched windows. Broad stone steps lead up to a raised covered entry portico. Key proportions: ~24 m x 24 m building base, 9 m main roof line, 15 m dome apex, and 22 m minaret spire. Palette: pure chalk white masonry (`#F4F4F4`), vibrant turquoise-cyan dome coating (`#1E828A`), warm sandy limestone plinth (`#E0D6C3`), and dark bronze window framing.

**Modeller Reproduction Guide:**
Main streetfront exterior elevation of a standard municipal Indonesian neighbourhood market hall (Pasar Perumnas Klender, East Jakarta, operated by PD Pasar Jaya). Modellers should capture the utilitarian concrete frame architecture with a distinct double-pitched corrugated metal clerestory roof designed for natural cross-ventilation. The building facade features a bold red-and-white header signage board ("PASAR KLENDER") mounted on weathered off-white and buff render. Ground-level retail bays feature distressed sky-blue and battleship-grey steel corrugated roll-up shutters (*rolling doors*), with concrete loading aprons cluttered with wooden fruit crates, plastic produce baskets, and parked automatic commuter scooters (Honda Beat/Vario style). Overhead low-voltage electrical drop lines add quintessential Jakarta urban realism. Proportions: two-storey building height of ~8.5 m, central entry portal width of ~6 m, and modular 3 m street shop bays. Palette: weathered municipal cream/buff (`#E5D9B6`), faded sky blue roll-up doors (`#5C97BF`), rusted corrugated roof panels (`#7C6258`), and bold municipal red signage (`#C62828`).

Interior central market aisle displaying traditional open-counter produce benches (*los pasar*). Overhead, an exposed light-gauge steel truss structure supports corrugated sheet roofing interspersed with translucent fibreglass skylight sheets that flood the hall with diffused daylight. Clutter elements to include: suspended bare fluorescent tube light fixtures, dangling mechanical spring balance scales, colourful hanging plastic tarpaulins (royal blue and yellow stripes) providing local stall sun/drip protection, plastic stools, and cardboard produce containers. Proportions: counter height 0.85 m, counter depth 1.2 m, aisle width 2.3 m, roof truss clearance 4.5 m. **Modeller Reproduction Guide:**
Ground-level perspective of transit departure platforms and roadway channels at Terminal Senen, Central Jakarta. Modellers should construct wide asphalt bus bays with crisp white painted stop boundaries, turning arrows, and lane limit lines. Flanking the lanes are raised concrete passenger boarding islands (20 cm kerb height) painted with high-visibility yellow-and-black diagonal hazard stripes, protected by tubular dark grey steel queue guardrails. Overhead stretches an industrial dark grey cantilevered steel canopy with trapezoidal profile roof sheeting, carrying suspended TransJakarta royal blue directional signs with white route numbers and terminal destination headers. Proportions: canopy roof clearance of 4.2 m to clear standard city transit buses; island platform width of 2.8 m; lane road width of 3.6 m per bus berth. Palette: asphalt tarmac grey (`#3B3E40`), safety hazard yellow (`#FFD700`) and pitch black (`#1A1A1A`), dark structural steel charcoal (`#2E3133`), and TransJakarta corporate blue signage (`#0066CC`).

The adjacent Mikrotrans angkot is built on a compact light-commercial van body (Suzuki Carry / Daihatsu Gran Max chassis) in signature JakLingko cyan/sky blue with a pure white roof, front bonnet route badge, side sliding passenger door, and rear tinted windows. ## 5. Pos Ronda (Neighbourhood Guard Post / Gardu Siskamling)

### Photo 1 — Traditional Timber Guard Post with Kentongan
- **Local File:** `reference/assets/pos-ronda-01.jpg`
- **Exact Commons Title:** `File:Pos Ronda Geneng, Jombang-3.jpg`
- **Direct Commons URL:** `https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c9/Pos_Ronda_Geneng%2C_Jombang-3.jpg`
- **Commons Page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pos_Ronda_Geneng,_Jombang-3.jpg
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0
- **Author:** Indonesiagood
- **Original Dimensions:** 3000 x 4000 pixels (5.93 MB)

**Modeller Reproduction Guide:**
Full architectural view of a quintessential Indonesian neighbourhood watch security post (*pos ronda* / *gardu siskamling*). 3D modellers should model a freestanding open-sided timber pavilion elevated on a 30 cm high rendered brick plinth. Four square corner timber posts support an open timber roof truss that carries an authentic pitched clay terracotta hip roof (*atap limasan*) with decorative ridge capping. Three sides of the post are enclosed by low horizontal wooden bench railings that double as perimeter seating for neighbourhood night watchmen. Suspended horizontally beneath the front roof eaves is the iconic hollowed-out bamboo or hardwood slit drum (*kentongan*) with its wooden striking mallet hanging beside it on a lanyard. The lower rendered plinth features vibrant civic two-tone painting (clean white with bright emerald green wainscoting) and displays a small wooden bulletin board for community announcements and duty rosters. Proportions: footprint ~2.5 m x 3.0 m, roof eaves clearance 2.2 m, total apex height 3.2 m from grade, bench seat height 0.45 m. Palette: whitewashed lime mortar (`#F4F4F4`), bright civic green wainscoting (`#0E8A4E`), stained hardwood/bamboo timber (`#8D5B36`), rustic terracotta roof tiles (`#C05A3E`), and weathered grey concrete base (`#7D8184`).
